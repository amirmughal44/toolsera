Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Our Commitment to User Privacy

At Toolsera ("we", "our", or "us"), operated and maintained by Adeel Ahmad, we believe in privacy by design. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to maintaining a transparent, non-intrusive environment across all our online tools and services.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike conventional online services:

  • No User Account or Registration: You do not need an account, password, phone number, or payment card to use Toolsera.
  • No Instagram Credentials: We never ask for, intercept, or handle your Instagram passwords or personal login cookies.
  • No Media Archival: Toolsera does not store, host, or archive downloaded videos, stories, or photos on our servers. All media streams are delivered directly through public Content Delivery Networks (CDNs).
  • No Tracking Fingerprints: We do not employ third-party behavioral profiling trackers.

3. Information We Collect Automatically

To maintain service integrity, guard against denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, and route high-volume traffic efficiently, our edge servers temporarily process minimal technical metadata:

  • Browser user-agent string and operating system version.
  • Anonymized IP address (used solely for rate limiting and fraud prevention).
  • Timestamp of requests and target URL format queries.

4. Feedback & Contact Inquiries

When you voluntarily submit feedback or report an issue via our website feedback form, you may choose to provide your name and email address. This information is used exclusively by developer Adeel Ahmad to respond to your technical request or bug report and is never shared, sold, or distributed to third parties.

5. Third-Party Links & Content Delivery

When downloading publicly available content, your browser establishes a secure connection to Meta Platforms, Inc. content servers. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of external third-party platforms.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical or legal improvements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Information

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact developer Adeel Ahmad directly at adeelahmed3376@gmail.com.
